About Sri Anantashayana Borewells & Pumps (HYDROWELL)

Founded in the year 2000, Sri Anantashayana Borewells & Pumps has stood as an industry pioneer across Karnataka, delivering end-to-end groundwater engineering with precision, integrity, and advanced hydraulic machinery.

Our field operations integrate scientific groundwater point identification by senior certified geologists, advanced hydraulic drilling rigs (4.5" & 6.5"), heavy-duty ISI casing pipes, and genuine submersible pumps (Texmo, CRI, Kirloskar). Whether drilling for independent residences, gated communities, farms, or commercial complexes, we ensure water security built to last.

Hydrogeological Point Survey

Scientific resistivity surveys to locate productive groundwater aquifers.

Class-D Casing & Filter Protection

High-tensile PVC and slotted filter pipes preventing borehole collapse and silt.

Submersible Motor Installation & Repairs

Precision pump lowering, electrical control panels, and 24/7 service backup.

How We Work

Our 4-Step Scientific Process

A structured engineering methodology guaranteeing water access and long-term borewell health.

01

Geological Survey

Senior hydrogeologists inspect your terrain, measure electrical resistivity, and mark optimal water coordinates.

02

Precision Drilling

High-powered hydraulic rigs drill through soft soil and granite formations using 4½" or 6½" industrial button bits.

03

Casing & Filter Insertion

Heavy-duty Class-D casing and slotted filter pipes are inserted into loose soil and fracture zones to seal against silt.

04

Motor Fitting & Testing

Stage-matched submersible pump lowering, water yield testing (GPH), control panel connection, and complete handover.
