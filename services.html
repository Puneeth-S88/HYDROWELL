Engineering Capabilities

Our Borewell & Pumping Services

End-to-end groundwater engineering from geologist survey to drilling, casing, pump installation, and maintenance across Karnataka.

Core Service

Borewell Drilling (4.5" & 6.5")

We operate advanced hydraulic sensor drilling rigs suitable for compact urban residential sites and large-scale agricultural farms. Depths up to 1,500+ feet with real-time geology observation.

Protection

Casing Pipe Installation

Installation of ISI certified Class-D PVC casing pipes and heavy-gauge mild steel (MS) casing pipes to protect against boulder collapse, surface runoff, and muddy water contamination.

Book Casing
Pumping

Submersible Pump Installation

Complete fitting of high-efficiency submersible pumps from leading manufacturers: Texmo, CRI, and Kirloskar. Single-phase and three-phase motor options with automated starter panels.

Book Pump
Filtration

Slotted Filter Pipe Screens

Factory-slotted PVC filter pipes installed at identified water veins to ensure smooth water inflow while stopping sand and silt from damaging the submersible pump motor.

Book Filter
Maintenance

Compressor Flushing & Cleaning

Revival and cleaning of old or choked borewells. High-pressure air flushing dislodges silt, sand buildup, and mineral scaling, restoring original water yield.

Book Flushing
Survey

Groundwater Geological Survey

Geophysical resistivity testing carried out by experienced hydrogeologists to map subsurface rock formations, identify fracture cavities, and determine precise drilling points.
