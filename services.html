What We Offer

Complete Service Range

Our experienced team provides geologist-guided borewell point marking, drilling, casing pipe installation, filter pipe installation and motor fitting services for residential, agricultural and commercial projects.

Borewell Drilling

Our borewell drilling service begins with identifying suitable water points through experienced geologists. Using advanced drilling equipment, we help customers establish reliable groundwater sources for long-term use.

Casing Pipe Installation

We install durable PVC and steel casing pipes that strengthen borewell walls, prevent collapse and improve the overall lifespan and performance of the borewell.

Submersible Pump Installation

We provide complete motor and submersible pump installation services, ensuring efficient water extraction, proper testing and reliable performance for years to come.

Pump Maintenance & Repair

Our maintenance services help keep your motor and pump systems operating efficiently while reducing downtime and unexpected repair costs.

Filter Pipes

We install high-quality slotted PVC filter pipes in the water-bearing zones of the borewell. These pipes allow clean water to enter while preventing sand and fine particles from entering the well, improving water quality and borewell performance.

Agricultural Borewell Solutions

We help farmers secure dependable water sources for irrigation, farming operations and agricultural development through professional borewell solutions.

Commercial Borewell Projects

Our team executes large-scale borewell projects for apartments, industries, educational institutions and commercial establishments with professional planning and execution.

Residential Borewell Services

From site inspection to motor installation, we provide complete residential borewell services designed to ensure a dependable household water supply.
