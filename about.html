Our Legacy

25+ Years of Certified Groundwater Excellence

Sri Anantashayana Borewells & Pumps (HYDROWELL) was established with a singular mission: to eliminate water uncertainty through rigorous engineering and transparent customer service.

Over the past 25 years, we have drilled and serviced more than 1,000 borewells across Karnataka. From densely built residential neighborhoods in Bengaluru to vast agricultural tracts in Mandya, Tumakuru, and Ramanagara, our team delivers consistent water yield with minimum environmental disruption.

Senior Hydrogeologist Point Marking

We do not drill blindly. Every point is verified via subsurface resistivity measurements to identify aquifer fractures.

Class-D Casing & Filter Protection

We install certified high-tensile casing pipes and slotted filter screens to stop cave-ins and silt intrusion.

Complete Submersible Motor Systems

Authorized installers of Texmo, CRI, and Kirloskar submersible pumps, complete with electrical starter panels and cables.

Machinery & Capabilities

Our Modern Rig Fleet & Equipment

Advanced high-pressure hydraulic rigs engineered for rapid penetration through hard rock and complex geological formations.

Heavy Hydraulic Drill Rigs

Equipped with high-volume air compressors capable of drilling up to 1,500+ feet in 4½" and 6½" bore diameters.

Scientific Geophysical Meters

Digital resistivity meters and underground water locators that map subsurface rock resistivity and water vein depths.

Compressor Flushing Units

High CFM air compressors specifically deployed for clearing choked borewells, silt removal, and borehole yield testing.
