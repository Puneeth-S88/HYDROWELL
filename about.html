Who We Are

Sri Anantashayana Borewells & Pumps

Sri Anantashayana Borewells & Pumps is a trusted name in borewell drilling and water solution services across Bengaluru and Karnataka. With more than 25 years of experience, we have successfully completed residential, commercial and agricultural borewell projects.

Every project is handled with careful planning, advanced drilling equipment and skilled technicians to deliver efficient borewell solutions that are built for long-term performance and customer satisfaction.

  • Geologist-Assisted Borewell Point Identification
  • Complete Borewell Drilling Solutions
  • Casing Pipe & Submersible Motor Installation
  • Filter Pipe Installation
  • Trusted Service Across Karnataka

Our Mission

Our mission is to help customers obtain reliable groundwater access through scientific borewell point selection, professional drilling practices and quality installation services that deliver lasting results.

Our Vision

To be Karnataka's preferred borewell partner by combining experience, technology and customer-focused service to create sustainable water solutions for future generations.

Our Values
